# Vehicle Insurance Claim Fraud Detection Dashboard
### Dashboard Link : https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/b1342259-35e2-42b8-891a-d355a46f9e83/c2eb66a0ab2720043ce7?experience=power-bi

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Vehicle Insurance Claim Fraud Detection Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** and insurance claim data to analyze fraudulent claim patterns, policy risks, customer demographics, and vehicle-related fraud indicators.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of insurance claim activities and helps identify suspicious claims through visual analytics. It enables insurance companies to monitor fraud rates, evaluate claim trends, and detect high-risk policy and vehicle categories for better decision-making.

---

## Aim of the Project

The primary aim of this project is to identify and analyze fraudulent vehicle insurance claims using data-driven insights and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard helps insurance companies:

* Detect potentially fraudulent claims.
* Monitor fraud rates across different policy types.
* Identify high-risk vehicle categories and price ranges.
* Analyze fraud patterns based on customer demographics.
* Improve claim verification processes.
* Reduce financial losses caused by fraudulent activities.
* Support risk management and strategic decision-making.

The ultimate goal is to transform raw insurance claim data into actionable insights that strengthen fraud prevention and operational efficiency.

---

## Project Objectives

* Analyze vehicle insurance claim fraud patterns.
* Monitor overall fraud rates and claim volumes.
* Identify policy types with higher fraud risk.
* Study fraud trends across different months.
* Analyze fraud distribution by gender.
* Evaluate fraud occurrence by vehicle make and category.
* Examine the relationship between vehicle age, price range, and fraudulent claims.
* Support insurance companies in fraud detection and risk assessment.

Male policy holders contribute the majority of fraudulent claims compared to female policy holders.

Certain vehicle manufacturers appear more frequently in fraud investigations, helping insurers prioritize risk assessment efforts.

---

### Vehicle Age Impact

Fraudulent claims are more concentrated among specific vehicle age groups, suggesting a relationship between vehicle age and fraud probability.

Claims are concentrated within particular vehicle price segments, highlighting potential fraud risk clusters.
